## What The 72 Traditional-Only Cases Are

All 72 traditional-only cases are HVRT cases where current SAC fails `reactive` while limit/survive
remain `NOT_EVALUATED` in the ODE proxy.

Main clusters:

- `swell_3ph`, `target_V_pu=1.1`, especially SCR 3/5/10/15 with 0.5 s and 1.0 s duration bins
- `swell_1ph`, `target_V_pu=1.2` at SCR 3
- `swell_1ph`, `target_V_pu=1.3` at SCR 5/10/15

Interpretation:

- These are shallow HVRT cases near the reactive-support evaluation boundary.
- Traditional or zero-action controllers often avoid a reactive `FAIL` because the event becomes
  `NOT_EVALUATED` for reactive demand.
- SAC sometimes produces a small wrong-sign reactive action, so the ODE proxy marks `reactive FAIL`.

This means current SAC is stronger in aggregate, but it does not yet strictly contain the pass-set of
all traditional baselines.

## Research Conclusion

Current pure SAC beats each traditional baseline in total proxy pass rate, but it has not yet proven
the desired dominance property:

> every scenario solved by dq/droop/fixed/MPC is also solved by SAC.

The remaining dominance gap is narrow and structured: 72 shallow HVRT reactive-sign cases.

Next SAC-only repair target:

- train `hvrt_sym` and `hvrt_asym` to output near-zero or correctly signed absorption in shallow HVRT
  regions where reactive demand is near the evaluation threshold;
- keep the final controller pure SAC, with no runtime rule layer;
- rerun this pass-set script after retraining and require `traditional_only_pass_count = 0`.
