Protected SAC fine-tune follow-up:

- `lab/results/hpt_protected_sacft_t2_a_hvrt105_20260725`
  tested chunked SAC fine-tuning with switch-level validation after each
  chunk.  Chunk 1 remained voltage-survival feasible but did not improve over
  the BC+DAgger baseline; chunk 2 failed due to recovery-envelope violation.
- `lab/results/hpt_protected_sacft_t2_a_hvrt105_20260725_tinyanchor`
  tested a much smaller update (`20` proxy steps per chunk) with stronger
  behavior anchoring.  All 8 chunks remained voltage-survival feasible, but the
  best score improvement over BC+DAgger was only `2.68e-9`, below the
  `1e-3` meaningful-improvement threshold.  Later chunks gradually worsened
  the score from `125.8460` to `126.4266`.
- This shows that switch-level chunk gating can prevent catastrophic SAC
  drift, but the current proxy SAC objective still does not provide a useful
  improvement gradient near the validated DAgger policy.
- `lab/results/hpt_trustregion_sacft_t2_a_hvrt105_20260726_seedsearch`
  added an `advance-policy=improve` trust-region gate.  All 10 candidates
  passed voltage-survival, but none improved over BC+DAgger; the policy was
  effectively frozen by the strong anchor.
- `lab/results/hpt_trustregion_sacft_t2_a_hvrt105_20260726_mediumanchor`
  relaxed the anchor while keeping switch-level chunk gating and rollback.
  Chunk 3 passed voltage-survival with zero timestep envelope, recovery, and
  fault-band violations, and improved the score from `125.8460` to `125.8084`
  (`0.0376` improvement).  Full FRT still failed on
  `gbt_recover;grid_current_limit`, so this is a voltage-survival policy
  improvement result, not full FRT certification.

## Interpretation Rules

- `feasibility_improvement` means the specialist passes voltage-survival while conventional fails.
- `quality_improvement` means both pass voltage-survival and the specialist has lower continuous score.
- `full_frt_pass` is not promoted by this package; current full FRT remains outside the L1 voltage-survival claim.
- Blank hashes or commands in `reproducibility_manifest.csv` are unresolved reproducibility gaps, not evidence.

## Updated Evidence Boundary

The current package supports the following bounded claim:

- Some switch-level-promoted, case-specialized policies achieve load-side
  voltage-survival and can beat the tested conventional baseline on selected
  cases.

The current package does not yet support the following stronger claims:

- full FRT certification;
- a unified SAC controller across all topology/fault cases;
- SAC fine-tuning contribution beyond BC/DAgger;
- a globally reliable proxy model;
- superiority over a fully tuned strong conventional controller.
