# Paper Workflow

Paper claims must trace to evidence.

Sections:

- Introduction: problem, HPT FRT challenge, limits of conventional dq/PI,
  motivation for RL.
- Method: HPT model, controller interface, RL formulation, safety constraints,
  training/evaluation pipeline.
- Experiments: scenarios, baselines, metrics, topology1/topology2 validation,
  generalization and ablation.
- Discussion: why RL helps, where it fails, proxy-to-Simulink gap, limitations.
- Conclusion: defensible contribution and future work.

Before drafting any quantitative claim, check that a result report or manifest
exists and name it in the paper notes.
